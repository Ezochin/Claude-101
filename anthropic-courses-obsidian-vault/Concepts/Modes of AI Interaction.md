# Modes of AI Interaction

Three modes describing how a human and AI relate on a given task. [[Delegation]] is the decision layer that picks which mode fits.

## Automation
AI executes a specific, well-defined task based on direct human instruction.

## Augmentation
Human and AI work together as thinking partners on a problem — a collaborative partnership, not a one-shot instruction.

## Agency
A human configures the AI *in advance* to act independently on their behalf going forward, like an autonomous agent. This is **not** the same as a human manually approving every micro-decision in real time — that would defeat the point of Agency mode. Handing off a high-stakes, hard-to-reverse decision (e.g. auto-approving refunds over $500) to Agency mode with zero human in the loop is a real risk worth interrogating: what's the cost of a wrong autonomous decision, and what audit trail or escalation path exists if something goes wrong?
