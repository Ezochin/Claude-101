# 4D Framework

## Definition (verified)
Anthropic's framework for working effectively with AI, made up of four competencies — **in this order**: [[Delegation]], [[Description]], [[Discernment]], [[Diligence]].

The four aren't a strict one-time sequence done in order and never revisited — they're interconnected and get cycled through repeatedly, often within minutes, on the same piece of work.

## The four competencies
- [[Delegation]] — deciding whether, when, and how to engage AI at all: what goes to the human, what goes to AI, what's shared
- [[Description]] — communicating goals clearly enough that AI produces something useful
- [[Discernment]] — accurately judging how good (and how correct) what came back actually is
- [[Diligence]] — taking responsibility for what you did with AI and how, including being honest about its role, and standing behind the result

## Course context
*AI Fluency for Builders* is the builder-specific, applied version of this framework — it lightly re-covers the 4Ds and recommends the foundational *AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations* course first for full depth.
