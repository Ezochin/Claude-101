# Delegation

## Definition (verified)
Part of the [[4D Framework]]. Deciding whether, when, and how to engage AI at all — what work goes to the human, what goes to the AI, and what the two do together (shared).

In practice this is a triage question: not every task deserves the same model or the same review depth. Low-risk, reversible tasks can get a cheaper model and light review; high-risk, hard-to-reverse tasks need the more capable model and real human review. Match effort to stakes, not habit.

## How it connects to the modes
Delegation is the decision layer that determines which [[Modes of AI Interaction|mode]] — Automation, Augmentation, or Agency — fits a given task.
