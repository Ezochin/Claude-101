# Artifacts

## Definition (verified)
A standalone, editable pane for one specific piece of content — a document, a piece of code, a diagram — that you can view, edit, and iterate on separately from the back-and-forth of chat, instead of digging through chat history to find and re-copy something.

## Why it matters
Solves the "I have to scroll back through a long chat to find the latest version" problem. Good for anything the person will keep revising or export elsewhere.
