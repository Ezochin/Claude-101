# Diligence

## Definition (verified)
Part of the [[4D Framework]]. Taking responsibility for what you did with AI and how you did it — including being honest about its role, and standing behind the result. Framed as owning the work from whether it should exist through whether it's still serving users after launch — not just a pre-shipping checkbox.

Doesn't get its own explicitly-named lesson inside "The 4D framework for builders" — it shows up later, under "Tying it all together," as **"Stand behind what you build."**

## Three named sub-areas
- **Creation Diligence** — being thoughtful about *which* AI systems or tools you choose to use in the first place, not just how you use them.
- **Transparency Diligence** — being honest with others about where AI was actually involved in the work (e.g. not quietly removing a "Powered by AI" disclosure because a stakeholder thinks it looks "less premium" — that's a trust and, in many jurisdictions, a compliance issue).
- **Deployment Diligence** — verifying, and being willing to vouch for, outputs before they go out the door (applies personally too — e.g. auto-applying to 200 jobs off one unread, generic resume).
